Privacy Policy

Social Hub (Demo) · Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect a TikTok account, we receive and store:

  • Your TikTok profile information: display name, avatar and account identifier (open_id).
  • Access and refresh tokens that let the service post on your behalf. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Videos and captions you upload to publish.

2. How we use it

We use this data only to show which account is connected and to publish the posts you choose to publish. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How long we keep it

Tokens are deleted when you disconnect your TikTok account. Uploaded videos are deleted after they are published or when you delete them. Test data may be removed at any time.

4. Your choices

You can disconnect your TikTok account from the service at any time, and you can also remove access from your TikTok app settings. To request deletion of your data, email us.

5. Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest and are never shown in the browser.

6. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: your-email@gmail.com
